About Us

/yoo-noy-uh/
Eunoia Beautiful thinking; a well-mind; goodwill.

Our mission is to foster a community where mental health is prioritized, understood, and supported without stigma. We believe that a well-mind is the foundation of a flourishing life.

Participation

You don't need to be a professional to make a difference. We are looking for empathetic individuals to help us organize local workshops, distribute mental health resources, or moderate our online community forums.
